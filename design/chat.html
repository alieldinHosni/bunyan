Ask panel

CoachChat, extended. It opens from the dock over the tab you are on, so a change can be seen landing behind it. Six states, English above Arabic.

Arabic

How it behaves

  • Opens from Ask, the fifth place on the dock, on every tab. The dock stays visible and usable.
  • Closes with ✕ or a tap outside. The outside tap only closes. No grabber, no drag.
  • ⤢ opens the whole history full screen, by a tap. It never grows by a drag.
  • The panel is as tall as what it holds, up to the top quarter of the screen, so a short answer leaves more of the tab in view.
  • The field is part of the panel, under the conversation, so it cannot cover an answer. With the keyboard up, the panel sits on the keyboard and the dock hides, as today.
  • An applied change scrolls the screen behind so the changed row is in view above the panel, outlined until the panel closes.
  • During a workout, Ask is in the ⋯ menu, never near the log control.

What changed from CoachChat

  • Answers lead with one or two sentences at 18px; today's detail is behind More.
  • Four new shapes, all inside the conversation: confirm, done with Undo, a question back, close matches.
  • Gone: the typing dots, the pulsing orb, the "Coach" label on every answer, the bubble tail.
  • No text over the background drawing: the panel is opaque, as the workout screen is.
  • A name that does not match is never answered with the nearest food. It gets close matches.
