What moved, and where

  • The 3D figure is off this screen. It stays behind ⓘ (How to do it), where the exercise sheet already shows it.
  • Tags (muscle, equipment, difficulty) go behind ⓘ. The dose stays as one line under the name.
  • "Last time" is said once, in the card, for the set you are on.
  • The personal-record card moves to Workout complete, where records are news.
  • Remove and Something hurts? are quiet links under the tools, not tiles beside them.
  • A warm-up is chosen when it is added (+ Warm-up), not toggled afterwards. Its row is dashed in gold with a W, and the card says it is not counted.
  • The rest no longer covers the screen. It sits above the next set, and the check logs from it.

The one row confirm

Delete stays on the left of a row and still asks. The question opens in the row itself instead of a sheet.
